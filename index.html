Leave categories
The kinds of leave people can pick, such as Sick or Casual. Whether a request is paid is chosen on the request itself and draws from the one paid allowance below, so a category is just a label.
CATEGORY
Paid allowance
Total paid days each person gets per year, usable in any category.
Signing in later than this asks for a reason. Signing in before the start, after the end, a second time the same day, or on a non-working day also asks for a reason.
Days the office is open. Signing in on any other day is treated as a non-working day (weekend or holiday).
Sign-in times, office hours, and reports use this timezone.
Holidays
Festivals and other days off. Leave requests skip these days, so a holiday inside a leave range does not use anyone's balance.
For a multi-day holiday like Eid, set a To date and each day in the range is added. No holidays added yet.
